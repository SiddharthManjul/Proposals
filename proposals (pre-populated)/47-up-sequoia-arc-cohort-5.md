BODY:
Sequoia Capital opened applications for Cohort 5 of its Arc program on May 5, 2026. Applications close June 16, 2026. Arc is Sequoia's structured pre-seed and seed founder development program, running on a biannual cadence with parallel cohorts in San Francisco and London.

## What it is

Arc is a ten-week structured program for pre-seed and seed founders. Each cohort runs simultaneously in San Francisco and London, with roughly `~15 to 20` companies admitted per location. The program features partner-led curriculum on product strategy, go-to-market, fundraising readiness and operating cadence. The investment terms are not publicly disclosed in the announcement and are aligned with Sequoia's typical seed-stage investments at each location.

Arc is positioned as a step-up from typical accelerator formats, with higher partner attention per cohort company and a network-density advantage from Sequoia's broader portfolio.

## Key dates and eligibility

- **Applications open.** May 5, 2026.
- **Applications close.** June 16, 2026.
- **Decisions.** Communicated by mid-July.
- **Program start.** Approximately August 24, 2026 in both locations.
- **Cohort 5 completion.** Approximately November 2, 2026.
- **Eligibility.** Pre-seed and seed-stage companies. Founders willing to participate in person in either San Francisco or London for the program duration. The SF cohort tends to admit a higher share of US-based teams. The London cohort skews toward European and UK-based teams plus a meaningful share of cross-border companies.

## Why it matters now

Arc has accumulated four prior cohorts of outcomes data, with public reports indicating Series A conversion rates above the typical accelerator baseline. By my read of the publicly visible alumni data, Series A close rates for Arc graduates run in the `~45 to 65%` range within twelve months of cohort completion, which compares favorably to typical seed-stage benchmarks of `~25 to 40%`.

For Cohort 5 specifically, the signal for founders considering applying is the same as for prior cohorts: the program's value-add comes from partner engagement density and the cohort itself. The opportunity cost for founders is approximately ten weeks of in-person commitment in either SF or London, which is a meaningful demand on founders whose customers, team or family are elsewhere.

The geographic constraint to SF or London remains the program's largest structural limit. Founders building in or for non-US-and-non-UK markets continue to face the implicit relocation cost. The cohort composition has historically been roughly `~30 to 40%` international, with the international participants disproportionately founders who could afford to relocate.

## Open questions

The Cohort 5 announcement does not address whether Sequoia is considering geographic expansion of Arc to additional regional nodes (Bangalore, São Paulo, Tel Aviv, Singapore are commonly speculated as candidates given Sequoia's global partner presence). Founders building outside the SF and London corridors who would benefit from a similar program structure should ask whether Sequoia's local partner teams have analogous programming on their roadmap.

The investment terms are not publicly stated in the cohort announcement, which is standard for Sequoia. Founders considering applying should expect terms aligned with Sequoia's typical seed-stage investments at each location, with the specific check size and equity discussed during the selection process.
